WatchSmart App Opening Animation

Goal

Create a premium app opening animation for WatchSmart.

The animation should feel like the brand is built from the core product interaction:

swipe cards → match/decision → WatchSmart logo appears

This should be used as an in-app splash/loading animation for now.

Later it can be reused for the native app launch animation.

⸻

Animation Concept

Two dark movie cards enter the screen from opposite sides.

They represent the swipe experience.

The backs of the cards move toward each other and meet in the centre.

When the cards meet, they snap/lock together.

The orange WatchSmart “W” logo appears on top.

Then the WATCHSMART wordmark fades in underneath.

Finally the tagline appears:

Less browsing. More watching.

Then the app transitions into Onboarding or Discover.

⸻

Animation Sequence

Duration

Total animation should be short:

1.5 to 2 seconds max.

Do not make users wait too long.

⸻

Step 1 — Dark background

Start with:

* full-screen dark/navy background
* subtle orange glow in centre
* no UI visible

⸻

Step 2 — Two card backs enter

Two card backs slide in:

* left card enters from left
* right card enters from right

Cards should look like premium movie cards:

* rounded corners
* dark navy/black
* subtle border
* slight poster texture or gradient
* soft shadow

They should not show real movie posters.

Use abstract card backs.

⸻

Step 3 — Cards meet

The two card backs move toward centre.

They slightly rotate into alignment.

When they meet:

* small scale bump
* subtle glow pulse
* tiny haptic-style visual snap

This should feel like the app has made a decision or match.

⸻

Step 4 — W logo forms

After the cards meet, the orange WatchSmart “W” mark appears on top.

Possible effect:

* W fades in
* W scales from 0.85 to 1
* orange glow briefly pulses
* cards behind it fade into the background

The W should be the hero.

⸻

Step 5 — Wordmark appears

Under the W, fade in:

WATCHSMART

Style:

* WATCH in white
* SMART in orange

Use official brand logo/wordmark if available.

⸻

Step 6 — Tagline appears

Under the wordmark, fade in:

Less browsing. More watching.

Style:

* “Less browsing.” in white/grey
* “More watching.” in orange

⸻

Step 7 — Transition into app

Fade the splash into:

* Onboarding if user is not logged in
* Discover if user is logged in

Do not block app loading unnecessarily.

If app data is ready before animation completes, still allow the animation to finish once.

If app loading takes longer, keep a subtle loading state after the animation.

⸻

Visual Style

Use:

* dark background
* orange glow
* premium card shadows
* smooth easing
* no cheesy bounce
* no childish animation
* no loud effects

The animation should feel cinematic, not cartoonish.

⸻

Technical Requirements

Implement as a reusable React component.

Suggested component name:

LaunchAnimation

Place in:

frontend/src/components/LaunchAnimation.jsx

or similar.

Use CSS animations or Framer Motion if already installed.

If Framer Motion is not installed, use CSS keyframes to avoid adding unnecessary dependencies.

⸻

Behaviour Rules

Show animation:

* on initial app load
* before onboarding/discover appears

Do not show animation:

* every time user changes tab
* every time user refreshes internal route
* after every login action unless it feels natural

Use session storage if needed so it appears once per session.

Example:

sessionStorage.setItem("watchsmart_launch_seen", "true")

⸻

Accessibility

Respect reduced motion:

If user has reduced motion enabled:

* skip card movement
* fade logo/wordmark in gently
* then continue

⸻

Acceptance Criteria

Animation is complete when:

1. Dark background appears.
2. Two card backs slide in from left and right.
3. Cards meet in the centre.
4. Orange W logo appears on top.
5. WATCHSMART wordmark fades in underneath.
6. Tagline appears underneath.
7. Animation lasts no longer than 2 seconds.
8. App transitions smoothly into Onboarding or Discover.
9. Animation appears only once per session.
10. Reduced-motion users get a simple fade version.
