DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Current issue:

WatchSmart still feels like a recommendation tool.

It needs to feel like a product.

The goal is:

Open app → Discover something → Save it → Watch it

in under 60 seconds.

Every screen should support that outcome.

GOLDEN RULES

Every screen must answer:

1. What should I watch?
2. Why am I seeing it?
3. What do I do next?

If a screen does not help answer one of those questions, simplify it.

WatchSmart should feel like:

“Netflix meets Tinder”

not

“TMDB with orange buttons.”
